Help Center

Find answers to common questions and get help with Retail Billing System.

Getting Started

Learn how to install and set up the application for the first time.

  • Download the APK file from our website
  • Enable "Install from Unknown Sources" in Android settings
  • Install the app and launch it
  • Create your account and complete the initial setup
  • Configure your business details and GST information

Troubleshooting

Common issues and their solutions.

  • App won't install: Check Android version (6.0+) and enable unknown sources
  • Data not syncing: Check internet connection and app permissions
  • Invoice not generating: Verify business details and GST settings
  • Voice assistant not working: Check microphone permissions
  • Reports not loading: Clear app cache and restart
