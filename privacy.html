Privacy Policy

ThynkTech India Private Limited, we are committed to protecting the privacy and personal data of users of the Retail Billing Software.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information.

Information We Collect

Business & Account Data

  • Business name, GST details, invoices, inventory records
  • Customer and supplier details entered by the User

Device & Technical Information

  • Device model
  • Operating system version
  • Masked phone number
  • Unique device identifiers

Permissions Used

  • Camera: To capture invoices and documents
  • Storage: To store invoices, reports, and backups
  • Contacts: Only to identify customers/suppliers already using the platform
  • Location: To verify business location and prevent fraud (not tracked in background)
  • SMS: Only for detecting transactional messages (OTP and personal SMS are never accessed)

Use of Information

We use collected data only for:

  • Providing and improving services
  • Billing and transaction processing
  • Security, fraud prevention, and analytics
  • Customer support and service notifications

Data Ownership

Users retain full ownership of all business data.
By using the software, Users grant ThynkTech India Private Limited limited rights to process data strictly for service delivery and internal operations.

Data Security

We use industry-standard safeguards including encryption, secure servers, firewalls, and restricted access controls. However, no system is completely secure, and absolute protection cannot be guaranteed.

Data Retention

  • Usage logs: 3 months
  • Transaction logs: 6 months
  • Event records: 1 year
  • Business data: Retained until deletion is requested by the User

Deletion requests can be raised via support email.

Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party services for analytics, infrastructure, and security. These providers follow strict confidentiality standards.

Cookies

Our website may use cookies to enhance user experience. Disabling cookies may limit some features.
