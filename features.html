Powerful Features for Your Business

Everything you need to manage your retail operations efficiently

🚀 Flagship

AI Voice Assistant

Add items to inventory and process sales using voice commands. Hands-free operation with intelligent speech recognition for faster transactions.

Invoice Management

Create and manage invoices with auto-generated invoice numbers. Support for Cash and Credit sales, print and share invoices as PDF, with invoice preview functionality.

Inventory Management

Real-time stock tracking, item management with units, prices, and tax rates. Stock alerts and management with barcode scanning support.

Party & Customer Management

Add and manage customers/suppliers, track party balances, customer statements, and GST details management.

Transaction Types

Estimate/Quotation, Sale Order, Delivery Challan, Sale Invoice. Convert between transaction types seamlessly.

Sales reports with date filters, revenue tracking, business wallet and credit wallet, transaction history.

Track business finances, credit management, payment tracking, and financial overview dashboard.

Customizable Settings

Transaction settings, invoice customization, tax and discount configurations, payment terms and due dates.

Complete retail billing solution for modern businesses. GST compliant, feature-rich, and easy to use.
