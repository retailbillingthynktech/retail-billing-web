Report an Issue

Help us improve by reporting bugs or issues you encounter.

How to Report

Send detailed information about the issue to our support team including:

App Version: App version

Include the version number from Settings → About

Android Version: Android version

Found in Settings → About Phone → Android Version

Steps to Reproduce: Steps to reproduce the issue

Provide detailed step-by-step instructions

Screenshots: Screenshots if possible

Attach screenshots or screen recordings if available

Email your report to:

support@retailbilling.ltd support@thynktech.ltd

Response Time

We aim to respond to all reports within 24-48 hours.

Initial Response:

Within 24 hours during business days

Resolution Time:

Depends on issue complexity, typically 2-5 business days

Priority Issues:

Critical bugs are prioritized and addressed immediately
